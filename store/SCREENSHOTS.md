# App Store screenshots — Culla

Culla is **universal** (`TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = 1,2`), so App Store Connect needs **two**
screenshot sets: one **iPhone** and one **iPad**. Both are required to submit.

## Required sizes (portrait)

**iPhone — 6.9" slot** (one of):
- **1320 × 2868** (iPhone 16 Pro Max), or
- **1290 × 2796** (iPhone 15/16 Pro Max) — either is accepted.

**iPad — 13" slot** (one of):
- **2064 × 2752** (iPad Pro 13", M4), or
- **2048 × 2732** (iPad Pro 12.9") — either is accepted.

- **Count**: 1–10 per set. Aim for **5–6** each.
- A single 6.9" iPhone set covers all smaller iPhones; a single 13" iPad set covers smaller iPads.

> Don't have a 6.9" iPhone or a 13" iPad? Capture on whatever you have and resize to the exact
> pixels above — tell me your shots' sizes and I'll resize them for you.

## How to capture (on your devices — no Simulator)

1. Open Culla, set up the shot (gallery colors, mode, background).
2. Press **Side button + Volume Up** together → it lands in Photos.
3. AirDrop / sync to your Mac.

> Tip: stage a few colorful galleries first — the sidebar's per-gallery colors and the dynamic
> mosaic background are what make Culla look alive in a thumbnail.

## Suggested 6-shot list (works for both iPhone and iPad)

1. **Hero — the swipe** — a photo mid-drag toward a brightly colored gallery, gallery names
   revealed, the dynamic mosaic glowing behind. Your most important shot.
2. **Galleries are real albums** — the galleries screen with several colorful galleries and the
   sort pill visible.
3. **Duplicate Sweep** — the side-by-side near-identical comparison.
4. **Insights** — the stats dashboard: totals, streak, the 7-day chart, reclaimed storage.
5. **On This Day / calendar** — the photo-mosaic calendar or the On This Day view.
6. **Make it yours** — Settings showing the accent palette / dynamic background, or a bold
   theme, to show the Living-Glass look.

(Optional: add a short caption above each shot in App Store Connect's media editor. Raw
screenshots are perfectly acceptable.)

### Design notes
- Same size, weight, and y-position on every shot in a set — reads as one family.
- Keep iPhone and iPad caption styling consistent.
